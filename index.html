About Me

A Little About Me

Turning ideas into functional, beautiful websites.

I'm Vinaya Vijay Ligam, a passionate Web Developer and WordPress Developer based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. With over 3 years of professional experience at Hub Tech Media Solutions, I specialise in building responsive, fast, and visually polished websites that genuinely work for my clients' goals.

My expertise spans the full front-end stack — from semantic HTML5 and modern CSS3 to WordPress theme customisation, plugin integration, and PHP. I've delivered websites across industries including business, healthcare, legal, educational, and corporate sectors, always keeping accessibility and user experience at the forefront of every project.

I'm deeply committed to clean code, pixel-perfect design execution, and on-time delivery. Whether building a site from scratch or redesigning an existing one, I bring care, craft, and precision to every line of code.
